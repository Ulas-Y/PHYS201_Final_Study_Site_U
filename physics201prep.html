PHYS 201 Core Law & Formula Cheat Sheet

Essential equations, vector relationships, and fundamental principles across all 10 exam problem domains.

1. Circuits (RC & RL)

  • RC Discharge: $V_C(t) = V_0 e^{-t/\tau}$, $\tau = R_{eq}C$
  • Stored Energy: $U_C = \frac{1}{2} C V^2 = \frac{Q^2}{2C}$
  • RL Growth: $I(t) = \frac{\mathcal{E}}{R}(1 - e^{-Rt/L})$, $\tau_L = \frac{L}{R}$
  • Inductor Energy: $U_B = \frac{1}{2} L I^2$
  • Power Balance: $\mathcal{E} I = I^2 R + \frac{dU_B}{dt}$

2. Lorentz Force & Particle Motion

  • Lorentz Force: $\vec{F} = q(\vec{E} + \vec{v} \times \vec{B})$
  • Velocity Selector: $v = \frac{E}{B_1}$ (when $\vec{F}_E + \vec{F}_B = 0$)
  • Cyclotron Radius: $r = \frac{mv}{qB_2}$
  • Impact Distance: $x = 2r = \frac{2mv}{qB_2}$
  • Mass Resolution: $\frac{\Delta x}{x} = \frac{\Delta m}{m}$

3. Ampère's Law & Superposition

  • Ampère's Law: $\oint \vec{B} \cdot d\vec{l} = \mu_0 I_{enc}$
  • Uniform Cylinder Inside: $\vec{B} = \frac{\mu_0 J}{2} \hat{z} \times \vec{r}$
  • Long Wire Outside: $B = \frac{\mu_0 I}{2\pi r}$
  • Toroid Field: $B(r) = \frac{\mu_0 N I}{2\pi r}$
  • Superposition Principle: Cavity = Full (+J) + Cavity (−J)

4. Faraday's Law & Motional EMF

  • Magnetic Flux: $\Phi_B = \int \vec{B} \cdot d\vec{A}$
  • Faraday's Law: $\mathcal{E}_{ind} = -\frac{d\Phi_B}{dt}$
  • Motional EMF: $\mathcal{E} = \oint (\vec{v} \times \vec{B}) \cdot d\vec{l} = B \ell v$
  • Lenz's Law: Induced $I$ creates flux opposing the flux change.

5. Maxwell-Ampère Law

  • General Law: $\oint \vec{B} \cdot d\vec{l} = \mu_0 I_{cond} + \mu_0 \varepsilon_0 \frac{d\Phi_E}{dt}$
  • Displacement Current: $I_D = \varepsilon_0 \frac{d\Phi_E}{dt}$
  • Parallel Capacitor ($r < R$): $B(r) = \frac{\mu_0 I_{cond} r}{2\pi R^2}$
  • Outside Capacitor ($r > R$): $B(r) = \frac{\mu_0 I_{cond}}{2\pi r}$

6. Biot-Savart & Dipoles

  • Biot-Savart Law: $d\vec{B} = \frac{\mu_0 I}{4\pi} \frac{d\vec{l} \times \hat{r}}{r^2}$
  • Circular Arc Field: $B_{center} = \frac{\mu_0 I \theta}{4\pi R}$
  • Magnetic Moment: $\vec{\mu} = N I A \hat{n}$
  • Torque & Energy: $\vec{\tau} = \vec{\mu} \times \vec{B}$, $U = -\vec{\mu} \cdot \vec{B}$
  • Small Oscillations: $\ddot{\theta} + \omega^2 \theta = 0 \implies \omega = \sqrt{\frac{NIAB}{I_{rot}}}$
